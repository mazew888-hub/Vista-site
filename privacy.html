Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

What Vista is

Vista is a personal feed reader that runs entirely on your own devices. There is no Vista server and no Vista account.

What stays on your device

Your feed list, folders, rules, saved posts and read history are stored in a local database on each device where you use Vista. Vista downloads feeds directly from the sites you follow.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync and sign in with Google, Vista asks only for the drive.file permission. That lets Vista create and read the files it makes itself (one file named vista_sync.json). It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.

The sync file holds your feed list, folders, rules, saved posts and recent read marks. It goes straight from your device to your own Google Drive. Nobody else, including the developer, receives it. The sign-in token is stored only on your device.

You can stop syncing at any time with "Sign out" in Vista, remove Vista's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the sync file from your Drive.

What Vista does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • Google user data is used only to provide sync, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
